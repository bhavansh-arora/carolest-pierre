03 — Wood therapy

Wood therapy

Specially designed wooden tools, like grooved rollers and the Swedish cup, sculpt and contour the silhouette while boosting blood and lymphatic circulation.

  • Supports lymphatic drainage and reduces puffiness
  • Improves the appearance of cellulite
  • Stimulates collagen and skin elasticity
  • Helps sculpt and define the silhouette
✦ Your plan

1 to 2 sessions a week, for 8 to 10 weeks.

Then roughly one maintenance session a month. Drink 1 to 1.5 L of water after each treatment and keep moving to extend the benefits. Frequency varies with your goals.
